## Voiceover script (Joshua)

1. if you've seen our proposal prep videos… well, she said yes. so now it's wedding prep time.
2. welcome to episode 1 of absolutlee i do, our wedding series. i flew to bangkok to get my wedding suits made. here's exactly how it went, how much it cost, and if it's worth it.
3. we landed on friday, and by 5pm i was at ricky's fashion house in sukhumvit, recommended by a friend.
4. i went in knowing exactly what i wanted: two suits. a classic black, single-breasted, and a bold burgundy, double-breasted with four buttons.
5. then measurements. waist, hips, legs, shoulders. every. single. inch.
6. the fun part is the lining. for the burgundy, they suggested pink flamingos to make the colour pop. for the black, a white and red pattern.
7. that same night, fitting one. they put you in a skeleton suit to check the cut.
8. saturday, fitting two. the pants were a bit long and the shirt a bit loose, so they adjusted both.
9. tip: if you're hitting the gym before the wedding, tell them. i asked for a little extra room.
10. monday, collection day. everything fit perfectly… and they even gave me a beer.
11. all in: two 2-piece suits, a shirt and two ties, alterations included, for S$1,038. that's about five hundred per suit.
12. would i recommend them? yes. friendly team, great advice, and they really know their suits. ep 2 coming soon.
